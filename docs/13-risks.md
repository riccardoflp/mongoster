# Risks and debt

What is known to be weak in Mongoster and not fixed yet. *Source: arc42 §11.* A **risk** may go wrong; **debt** is
already wrong and costs on every change. Most rows were found while writing the cards, in the code, and are not
reproduced by a test yet: the card or the place in the code says where. RS24 to RS46 were found by the security
analysis of 0.10.0. [07-roadmap.md](07-roadmap.md) says when each is dealt with; a row is removed in the commit
that fixes it, with a test that failed before.

*How likely* is a judgment: *often* (a normal use reaches it), *sometimes* (a less common setup), *rarely* (a damaged
file, a race).

## Losing data or writing where it must not

| | Kind | What | If it bites | How likely | What we do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RS1 | risk | When the undo history's `index.json` cannot be read, `load` treats it as empty and the pruning that follows deletes every `*.json.gz` ([11-data.md](11-data.md#versions)). | The whole undo history is gone, without a warning. | rarely (a damaged or older-format file) | M3: keep the files and the damaged index aside, warn |
| RS2 | risk | When `scheduledTasks/tasks.json` cannot be read, it counts as empty and the next change to a task writes only that task. | Every other scheduled task is lost, without a warning. | rarely | M3: refuse to write over a file that could not be read, warn |
| RS3 | risk | Each VS Code window keeps its own list of undo operations and rewrites `index.json` from it, without a lock (`undo.record`). | Operations of one window are dropped by another, and their files deleted at the next start: those writes can no longer be undone. | often, with several windows | M3: re-read the index under the lock of `src/fileLock.ts` before writing |
| RS8 | risk | A document, a view definition or validation rules opened in an editor tab keep the read-only state the connection had when they were opened (`ro=1` in the URI): after **Make Read-Only**, a save still writes (`document.edit-in-editor`, `manage.edit-view`, `manage.edit-validation`, `connection.read-only`). | A write through a connection the user made read-only, perhaps production. | sometimes | M3: check the connection at save time |
| RS9 | risk | JSON Lines export reads with `promoteValues` on, so a whole Double (42.0) is written as `$numberInt` and comes back as an Int32 (`transfer.export`); scheduled exports too. | Types change through an export and an import, against the product's promise. | often | M3 |
| RS10 | risk | `Duplicate` copies the relaxed row of the table: whole Doubles and safe Longs become Int32, and fields left out by the projection are not copied (`document.duplicate`). Inline edits keep only `Long` as an explicit type (`document.keep-numeric-types`). | A duplicated or edited document has other types, or fewer fields, than the user expects. | often | M3 |
| RS11 | risk | Some confirmations do not say that the write cannot be undone: `Delete`, `Update many` and `Delete many` with the undo history off; dropping or rebuilding an index; an operation over 20 MB known only after reading (`document.delete`, `document.update-many`, `document.delete-many`, `undo.record`, `index.edit`, `index.drop`, `index.drop-search`). | The user expects an undo that does not exist. | sometimes | M3 |
| RS13 | risk | When one operation's file is larger than `mongoster.history.maxSizeMB`, pruning deletes it and every older operation (`undo.limits`). | Older operations that fit are lost too. | rarely | M3 |
| RS4 | debt | A dropped collection's query history, saved queries for me, layout, expanded columns and formatting rules stay in `globalState`, and come back if a collection of the same name is created. A collection's color goes with a drop from Mongoster (`tree.collection-color-follows`), but stays after a drop done elsewhere, as these keys do. | Stale state; growth of `globalState`. | often | M5: M2's colors of collections did not take the other keys with them, because saved queries must not be lost |
| RS38 | risk | A playground on a read-only connection still runs `db.runCommand({ profile: … })` and `{ validate: …, repair: true }` (`READ_COMMANDS` in `src/playground/shell.ts`). | The profiler's level changes and `system.profile` is created, or a repair rewrites data, through a connection the user made read-only. | rarely | M3: `profile` only to read the level, `validate` without `repair` |

## Secrets and privacy

| | Kind | What | If it bites | How likely | What we do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RS5 | risk | The desktop app without a keychain stores secrets in `secrets.json` as `plain:<value>`, without telling the user (T2, `connection.secrets`). | Connection strings with passwords in clear text in the user's profile. | sometimes (Linux without a keyring) | M3: warn, and ask before storing in clear text |
| RS6 | risk | `Without passwords` and the passphrase leave `proxyPassword`, `AWS_SESSION_TOKEN` and, in two formats, `tlsCertificateKeyFilePassword` in clear text in exported files (T1, `connection.export`). | A credential leaks through a file the user believed safe to share. | sometimes | M3 |
| RS7 | risk | The connection form receives the connection string and the Redis URL with their passwords (T3, `connection.form`). | A secret is in a webview's memory; the README says it never is. | often | M3: send them without the password, keep the saved one when the field is left empty |
| RS15 | risk | `list_connections`, `list_databases`, `list_collections` are not bounded by `MAX_OUTPUT` (`mcp.output-limits`). | A slow or huge answer to the AI. | rarely | M4 |
| RS16 | risk | Files read from outside (imports, connection files, backups) are read whole, without a size limit (T5). | A huge or crafted file makes Mongoster run out of memory. | rarely | M5 |
| RS17 | risk | Playgrounds run in any workspace, trusted or not (T6, Q8). | Code from someone else's repository runs with the user's connections when the user runs it. | rarely | M5, after Q8 |
| RS24 | risk | A field path with `__proto__` in an oplog entry reaches `Object.prototype` when a document's history is built (`setPath`, and the diff and revert functions, of `src/shared/docHistory.ts`). | Whoever can write to a database the user browses changes the prototype of every object in the extension host, which other extensions share. | sometimes | M3, first: refuse the path segments `__proto__`, `constructor` and `prototype`, and step into own properties only |
| RS25 | risk | A CSV header with `__proto__` does the same when the file is parsed, before the `Import` confirmation (`setPath` of `src/shared/csv.ts`, `transfer.import`). | A file from someone else changes the prototype of every object in the extension host. | sometimes | M3, with RS24: refuse such a header with an error |
| RS26 | risk | The bundles `require` the optional modules of the driver and of ssh2 by bare name (`optional` in `esbuild.mjs`; `cpu-features` at every start), and none is shipped: Node looks for them in every `node_modules` above the app, which for the AppImage is `/tmp/node_modules` and on Windows `C:\node_modules` (T19). | Another account of the machine has its code run inside Mongoster, with the secrets. | rarely (a shared machine) | M3: each optional module replaced in the bundle by a stub that throws; a check that the bundles have no bare `require` left |
| RS27 | risk | With `mongoster.mcp.readDocuments` off, a string that starts with `$` is kept as if it were a field reference (`shapeOf` in `src/shared/profile.ts`), a command cut by the server (`$truncated`) is shown as it is (`commandLine` in `src/mcp/performance.ts`), and the numbers and booleans of a `sort` or `projection` are kept (T9, `mcp.read-documents`). | Password hashes (bcrypt, argon2, crypt), prices and pieces of other clients' commands reach the AI through `slow_queries`, `current_operations`, `index_advice` and `list_saved_queries`. | sometimes | M3: keep only what has the form of a field reference; a placeholder for `$truncated` |
| RS28 | risk | The refusal of `system.*` collections does not cover `local.oplog.rs`, read by name or joined (`isSystemCollection` in `src/mcp/context.ts`, T9). | With `readDocuments` on and a user who reads `local`, the oplog's entries of `admin.system.users` and `admin.system.keys` reach the AI: credentials. | rarely | M3: refuse the oplog collections of `local` in `target` and `joinedCollections` |
| RS29 | risk | A connection file encrypted with a passphrase encrypts the secrets only: the connection string, the SSH host and the Redis host beside them can be changed by whoever has the file, without the passphrase, and the import list shows neither `authMechanism` nor the paths of the TLS and SSH files (T1, T5, `connection.export`, `connection.import`). The key derivation is the one of the Compass format (PBKDF2, 100 000 rounds). | The user imports with the right passphrase and the password goes to someone else's server. | rarely | M3, after a proposal (a file format changes): a new version of Mongoster's file whose encryption covers the hosts, with a slower key derivation; `authMechanism` and the file paths in the import list |
| RS30 | risk | `open_search` runs the caller's regular expression in the extension host on every string of every document, with no limit of time, and with `limit: 0` on a whole connection (`src/mcpTools.ts`, `src/search.ts`, T9). | A text steering the AI blocks Mongoster and the MCP server, while every collection is read. | rarely | M3: for the MCP server, a collection or a limit is required, and the matching has a time budget |
| RS31 | risk | The default name of a connection is cut from the connection string at the first `/` or `@`, so a password with a `/`, or a string without a host, puts the user and part of the password in the name (`hostOf` in `src/shared/connectionsFile.ts` and `src/connectionEditor.ts`, R14.1). | Part of a password in `globalState`, the tree, tab titles and `list_connections`. | rarely | M3: the host after the last `@`; `MongoDB` when what is left is not a host |
| RS32 | risk | A password with an `@` in a Redis URL is split at the first `@` by the export and at the last by the connection (`userInfo` in `src/shared/connectionsFile.ts`, `connection.export`). | `Without passwords` and the passphrase leave the end of the password in clear text in the file. | rarely | M3, with RS6 |
| RS33 | risk | The driver's error for a connection string it cannot parse quotes the whole string; the error is shown, returned by the MCP server and written to `Mongoster MCP` (`errorMessage` in `src/tree.ts`, `src/shared/mcp.ts`, T13). | A password in a notification, an answer to the AI and a log. | rarely | M3: the user info of `mongodb://` and `redis://` URLs removed from every error text |
| RS34 | risk | A proof of having the MCP token is accepted by the window that made it: `requestRelease` does not record its own nonce (`ProofChecker` in `src/mcp/http.ts`, T8). | A program that took the port sends the proof back within 30 seconds and the window stops its server, saying another window runs it. | rarely | M5: a window refuses the proofs it made |
| RS35 | risk | Generated Python, Java, C# and Go code writes the text of a `$numberLong` as it is (`src/shared/codegen.ts`). | A saved query from someone else's repository puts code in the source the user copies and runs. | rarely | M5: only digits, or an error |
| RS36 | risk | CSV export writes a cell that starts with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` as it is (`csvValue` in `src/shared/csv.ts`, `transfer.export`; scheduled exports too), and the desktop app opens a `.csv` of GridFS with the system's program (`src/shared/openable.ts`, T16). | A value written by someone else is a formula in the user's spreadsheet. | sometimes | M5, after a question in [08-decisions.md](08-decisions.md): an option that prefixes such cells, since it changes what an import reads back |
| RS37 | risk | The check that `mongoster.savedQueries.folder` stays in the workspace compares names, not what a symbolic link leads to (`staysInWorkspace` in `src/projectQueries.ts`, T17). | A repository with a link in place of `.mongoster/queries` makes Mongoster read, write and delete query files outside it. | rarely | M5: compare the real paths |
| RS39 | risk | The local port of an SSH tunnel accepts any program of the machine (`src/transport.ts`). | On a machine with other users, another account reaches the deployment behind the SSH server. | rarely | M5: a proxy with a user and a password made for each tunnel |
| RS40 | risk | Backups, exports and files saved from GridFS are created with the system's default permissions, not the 0600 of the undo history ([11-data.md](11-data.md)). | On a machine with other users, copies of the data can be read by them. | sometimes | M5 |
| RS41 | risk | On Linux without a keyring, Electron may keep secrets as `basic_text`, where `safeStorage.isEncryptionAvailable()` is true and the key is fixed (`src/standalone/vscode/storage.ts`, T2). Not checked on such a session. | Secrets that look encrypted and are not: RS5 without its `plain:`. | sometimes | M3, with RS5: check it, and treat `basic_text` as no keychain |
| RS42 | risk | The MCP server accepts `$where`, `$function` and `$accumulator` in filters and pipelines (`src/mcp/context.ts`), which T9 does not count as a plain read. | JavaScript from the AI runs on the server, up to `MAX_TIME_MS`. | rarely | M5: refuse them |
| RS43 | risk | In the desktop app a tab's frame may navigate to the shell's address, and the permission check compares the start of the origin (`src/standalone/main.ts`). No way to use either is known. | With another flaw, a frame gets what the shell can do. | rarely | M5: frames stay on their own address; the origin compared whole |
| RS46 | risk | `watch_changes` has no `maxTimeMS` on its first read, and `find_documents`, `describe_collection` and `describe_relations` bound the number of documents they hold, not their size (`src/mcp/changes.ts`, `src/mcp/data.ts`, `mcp.output-limits`). Not checked against a server. | A call that hangs or takes much memory. | rarely | M4, with RS15 |

## Things the user sees wrong

| | Kind | What | If it bites | How likely | What we do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RS12 | debt | After an undo, open collection tabs are not refreshed; cancelling the undo's confirmation leaves the button on `Undoing…` (`undo.revert`). After a copy, a restore or a comparison's write, open tabs of the target are not refreshed (`transfer.copy-collection`, `backup.restore`, `compare.copy-document`). | The tab shows documents that are no longer so. | often | M3 |
| RS18 | debt | Removing a connection leaves its collection tabs, playground result and editor tabs open (`connection.remove`); the playground result tab does not take a new color at once (`connection.set-color`). | Tabs that fail on their next query. | sometimes | M5 |
| RS19 | debt | The comparison's writes have no `Undo` in their notification, although they are recorded (`compare.copy-document`, `compare.make-like`). | The undo exists but is hard to find. | sometimes | M5 |

## The code and its checks

| | Kind | What | If it bites | How likely | What we do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RS20 | debt | No tests for `src/history.ts`, `src/manage.ts`, `src/definitionFs.ts` and the host classes in general; many cards say **Tested by**: *none*. | RS1 to RS13 went unnoticed; a change to the undo history is unchecked. | — | M3: a test with each fix; then by area in M5 |
| RS21 | debt | Mongoster's own file formats have no version field (Q3, R15.1). | The first change of format cannot tell old files from damaged ones (see RS1, RS2). | — | M3, with RS1 and RS2 |
| RS22 | debt | No linter: the import rules of R1.2 and R1.3 for modules no webview imports, and the size limits of R6.4, are checked by review only (Q4). | Layers leak; files keep growing. | — | M4 |
| RS23 | debt | `catalog.py` is not in the CI (Q6). | Cards drift from the code without anyone noticing. | — | M4 |
| RS44 | debt | The workflows name their actions by tag, not by commit; nothing proposes updates of dependencies; `npm audit` is not in the CI. Today: monaco-editor 0.57 ships a DOMPurify with known flaws in the desktop app, and Electron is one major version behind. | A changed action runs in a release (T15); a known flaw stays in what is shipped. | — | M4: actions pinned to commits, updates proposed by a bot, `npm audit` of what is shipped in `build.yml` |
| RS45 | debt | [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md) names 0.8.x as the supported version and has no private channel for reports (Q7). | A flaw found by someone else has nowhere to go but a public issue. | — | M4: answer Q7; the version read from `package.json` by the release check |
